Model

Each regular agent i replaces its opinion by the average of its own and of its graph neighbours j with ‖xᵢ − xⱼ‖ ≤ σᵢ. Stubborn agents have σ = 0 and never move, but others listen to them. The paper proves that every trajectory converges when all regular agents share one bound (Theorem 5.1), and that the interaction graph stops changing after finitely many steps in dimension one (Theorem 5.7) and for almost every stubborn profile in higher dimensions (Theorem 5.5).

The energy 𝒱 is the sum over edges of min(‖xᵢ − xⱼ‖², σ²). With a common bound it decreases at every step (Lemma 3.1); with different bounds it need not.
